# 012 — Deterministic arcade contact with visible scenery

Status: specified; actual CI evidence and device testing pending.

## User story and scope
As a young driver, I can steer into the fictional city but should not clip directly through the largest buildings or the small visible roadside kiosk. When a collision occurs, the truck stops safely and I can reverse or press R to recover.

## Integrity
- The scene's occupied footprints and the simulator's blocking volumes MUST share one source of truth. No invisible giant walls; no fake true rigid-body collisions.
- Approximate truck as an oriented 2D rectangle based on physical 2.50m body width, 8.387m body length **plus measured 0.645m front bumper**, preserving actual front offset. Keep X/Z alignment and -Z-forward physics.
- Use bounded deterministic fixed-timestep contact checks that handle crossings between endpoints; no NaN, hangs, or teleport through a thin barrier. Collision stops linear velocity; no torque, bounce, tire/suspension or height-aware force simulation.
- Imported GLBs still use the same *calibrated placeholder truck footprint*, and actual model dimensions/orientation must be parent-verified.
- No browser telemetrics, third-party physics package, source HEIC changes, or camera-coordinate changes.

## Requirements
### FR-001 Shared geometric scene
Given city buildings shown in WebGL, when a collision is checked, then all solid building footprints are generated by the same deterministic data used for rendering. The road and scenery markers don't block unseen space. Spawn at x=1.75,z=0 must remain unblocked.

### FR-002 Stop before entering a footprint
Given finite current/proposed vehicle states, when the rotated calibrated truck would overlap a building at any swept intermediate position, then its motion stops at the last collision-free position and speed is zero. Straight, side and diagonal orientations work; measured forward bumper is included.

### FR-003 Recoverable behavior
Given collision contact, when the driver reverses away or resets, then motion can resume without corrupting heading/camera/speed; show brief understandable user-visible collision feedback and R hint, without a React setState per frame.

### FR-004 Preserve existing driving
Given straight unobstructed road, when normal keyboard/touch acceleration occurs, then forward motion stays intact with same drive model and deterministic step. Old keyboard, touch, drivetrain and garage E2E must continue passing.

### FR-005 Evidence and limits
Given CI, when tests run, then SAT/swept-contact pure Node tests and a Chromium sample collision/recovery acceptance prove bounds and UI response. Real Apple-device physical feeling and imported-child-GLB profile are manual. This is **not Rapier rigid-body physics**.

## Non-goals
Curbs/sidewalk tire physics, per-wheel ground contacts, springs, sliding/bouncing against walls, traffic cars, polygonal roads, hills or general glTF triangle collider generation.
