# Kid-first 3D interaction principles

1. **Immediate affordance**: show exactly how to steer, brake, reset; controls stay readable over bright and dark scenes.
2. **Undo and recover**: reset car returns safely to spawn; no punishments or irreversible clicks.
3. **Predictable motion**: smoothly capped acceleration, steering and chase camera. Never surprise the driver with teleportation.
4. **Sensory respect**: music requires manual play; user has pause/volume; future reduced-motion and audio toggles.
5. **Believable proportions**: one world unit equals a meter; road lane 3.5 m; road signs/buildings follow road scale. Digitized toy can be uniformly 'grown' to a driveable car without stretching LEGO proportions.
6. **Realism boundaries**: separate authentic child car from stylized environment; label Los Angeles inspiration clearly, no fake street accuracy.
7. **Guardians in control**: uploads stay local; no public sharing, chat or microtransactions.
8. **Visual target**: soft daylight, readable silhouette, approachable realistic materials; avoid expensive postprocessing before stable driving. Later add optional high/low graphics.
